# Impulsive — Early-stage behaviour-change app for impulse interruption

**Website:** https://useimpulsive.com

---

## 1. What Impulsive is

Impulsive is an early-stage behaviour-change app designed to help people create a pause between urge and action.

When a difficult moment arrives — a craving, a compulsion, an impulsive pattern — most people have very little structured support in that exact window. Impulsive is being built to fill that gap: not with motivation, not with streaks, but with a calm, structured interruption that gives the user a moment to notice, reflect, and choose differently.

The app focuses on three things:

- **Noticing** — recognising that a risky moment is happening
- **Interrupting** — using a guided pause to create distance from the urge
- **Redirecting** — offering a replacement action that supports recovery rather than shame

It is not a therapy app. It does not diagnose. It does not replace professional support. It is a structured tool for private, everyday moments.

---

## 2. The problem

The hardest moment in any habit loop is not the craving itself — it is the few seconds between the urge and the action. That is the moment most tools ignore.

Existing solutions tend to fall into one of three patterns:

- **Streak-based apps** that punish a slip and increase shame
- **Passive tracking tools** that observe behaviour without intervening in the moment
- **Therapy platforms** that are valuable but not available at the moment of impulse

Impulsive is exploring a different approach: structured, calm interruption in that specific window, without attaching shame, judgment, or identity to the outcome.

---

## 3. The approach

The product is being built around several core ideas:

- **Immediate pause/intercept flow** — triggered at the moment of urge, not after the fact
- **Reflection prompts** — short, grounded questions that slow the moment down
- **Replacement actions** — small, accessible behaviours offered as alternatives
- **Path-based support** — structured support across three areas: mind, body, and soul, recognising that impulse patterns often have physical, psychological, and emotional components
- **Nexus** — a coordinating layer that helps the user understand their own patterns over time, gently and without judgment
- **Privacy-first design** — no social features, no public progress, no exposure of sensitive behaviour

The current design direction is exploratory and is being refined in response to expert feedback.

---

## 4. Current stage

Impulsive is early-stage. There is no clinical validation and no published research backing at this point.

What exists:

- A live website at useimpulsive.com describing the concept and direction
- A waitlist for people interested in early testing or release
- A working product concept, actively being refined

The founder is deliberately not making clinical claims and is seeking expert input before the product moves into broader use or public health contexts.

---

## 5. What feedback is being requested

The founder is looking for honest, critical input from people with relevant expertise. Specifically:

- **Is the framing clinically safe?** Does the language used on the website or in this summary make any claims that are inappropriate or misleading?
- **Is the approach responsible?** Are there psychological or clinical risks in the pause-and-redirect model that need to be addressed?
- **What should be avoided?** Are there patterns in behaviour-change apps that cause harm — and that Impulsive may be at risk of replicating?
- **What would strengthen credibility?** What research, frameworks, or clinical perspectives would make this more grounded and trustworthy?
- **Would the advisor be open to a short feedback call?** Even 20 minutes of honest input would be genuinely valuable at this stage.

There is no expectation of endorsement. Honest critical feedback is more useful.
