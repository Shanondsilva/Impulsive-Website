**Subject:** Early feedback request — Impulsive, a behaviour-change app concept

Hi [Name],

I hope this finds you well. I'm reaching out because I'm building something that I think could benefit from your perspective, and I wanted to ask if you'd be willing to share a small amount of your time.

I'm the founder of Impulsive — an early-stage behaviour-change app I'm building to help people create a structured pause between urge and action. The idea is to intervene in the specific window between a craving or impulse and the action that follows, using a calm, guided interruption rather than motivation, streaks, or shame-based mechanics.

A few things I want to be transparent about upfront:

- This is genuinely early-stage. There is no clinical validation, no published research backing, and no large user base yet.
- I am not making medical claims. Impulsive is not a therapy replacement, not a medical device, and not intended for crisis intervention.
- I'm building carefully. I'd rather take longer and get the framing right than move fast and cause harm.

The reason I'm reaching out to you specifically is that your work in [relevant area — e.g. compulsive behaviour / habit research / clinical psychology / addiction recovery] is directly relevant to the space I'm working in, and I think honest expert input at this stage is worth far more than any amount of iteration without it.

What I'm asking for is not an endorsement. I'm asking whether the framing is responsible, whether there are risks I'm not seeing, and whether there are things I should avoid or address before the product reaches users more broadly.

If you have 20 minutes for a short call, I'd genuinely value the conversation. Alternatively, even a few lines of written feedback would be helpful.

The website is live at https://useimpulsive.com if you'd like a sense of the current direction before deciding.

Thank you for considering it. I understand your time is limited and I don't take that lightly.
