The problem

Most habit apps make one off-plan moment feel like off-plan moment.

Many tools rely on pressure, streaks, hard blocking, and judgement. Impulsive is designed differently. It helps users pause, redirect, and review progress without turning a difficult moment into an identity.

The Impulsive loop

A calmer pivot loop.

01

Notice the pattern

Understand your recurring cues, time windows, emotions, and environments with private reflection.

02

Pivot through the moment

Use a soft prompt, short exercise, movement reset, or focus shift when the habit loop begins to accelerate.

03

Reduce the loop over time

Review what helped, taper intensity, and keep moving without streak pressure or perfection rules.

Progression, not overwhelm

Start tiny. Unlock strength.

The first experience is one difficult-moment pause, one recommended action, one progress bar, and one clear next unlock. Premium and referrals belong in calm progress moments, never when someone is trying to get help quickly.

How it works

Designed for the moment before the decision.

  1. Step 1

    Set your pattern

    Name the habit loop, its likely trigger windows, and a realistic taper target.

  2. Step 2

    Receive a calm intervention

    Get one private recommendation first, with fallback support if the moment needs a different route.

  3. Step 3

    Review without judgement

    Reflect on what happened and what helped, without punishment, inflated scores, or public pressure.

Supporting feature

Focus Mode helps you return without friction.

Focus Mode supports the loop around support: start focus, recover from pause, and resume smoothly. It stays separate from Synchrology, which coordinates the support paths behind the scenes.

Principles
