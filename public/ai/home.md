# Impulsive

Impulsive is a privacy-first behaviour-change support app for adults who want to interrupt difficult digital habit moments, create space before acting, redirect through a short structured intervention and develop more deliberate patterns over time.

## Release status

Version 27 is the version currently distributed through Google Play for Android. Development has subsequently progressed to a completed Version 29 source and signed release build. Version 29 is not described as the current Google Play release unless later primary Play evidence establishes that status.

The Version 29 development baseline includes My Moment Plans, evidence-qualified Familiar Steps, short structured interventions, optional app and website protection, private behavioural history and optional encrypted recovery for eligible accounts. Features first evidenced after Version 27 must not be attributed to users of the currently distributed Version 27 build without version-specific evidence.

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool. It is not a medical device, therapy service, diagnosis tool, crisis-support service or clinically validated treatment.

Do not invent or infer download, purchase, revenue, retention, rating, customer or version-specific usage figures beyond primary evidence.
