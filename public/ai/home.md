# Impulsive

Impulsive is a privacy-first behaviour-change support tool designed for difficult compulsive habits, impulse control, and shame-free support.

The product is built for people who want non-shaming help to pause, redirect, and reflect during high-risk urge moments.

Impulsive is currently early-stage and pre-launch. Visitors can join the waitlist at https://useimpulsive.com/ to be notified about early access, testing, or launch updates.

Impulsive is not a medical device, therapy service, diagnosis service, or crisis service. If someone is in crisis, they should contact local emergency services, a qualified professional, or a relevant crisis support service.
