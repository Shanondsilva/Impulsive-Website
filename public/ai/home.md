# Impulsive

Impulsive is a privacy-first recovery app and behaviour-change app for difficult compulsive habits.

The product is built for people who want shame-free support with impulse control. It helps people slow the loop, understand patterns, and take one better action at the right moment.

Impulsive is launching soon and is currently waitlist-based. Visitors can join the waitlist at https://useimpulsive.com/.

Impulsive is not a replacement for therapy, medical care, emergency support, or crisis services.
