# Product

Impulsive is a privacy-first behaviour-change support tool designed for difficult compulsive habits, impulse control, and shame-free support. The product is currently early-stage and pre-launch.

The product is designed around recovery paths:

- Mind: pause, name the pattern, and choose a calmer next move.
- Body: change state through movement when thinking alone is not enough.
- Soul: optional grounding through reflection, values, prayer, or recommitment.
- Nexus: a planned future feature designed to coordinate support across recovery paths. Nexus is not a current live capability; it is described on the site as a "Coming later" feature.

Some screens, metrics, and recovery indicators shown on the marketing site are prototype previews with example data, not real user outcomes or clinical results.

Impulsive is launching soon. It is not available through app stores yet, and no availability claims, ratings, downloads, or clinical outcomes should be reported.

Impulsive avoids shame, panic design, fake recovery scores, and paywalls during trigger moments. Impulsive is not a medical device, therapy service, or crisis service.
