# Product

Impulsive is a privacy-first recovery app for difficult compulsive habits. It is also a behaviour-change app focused on impulse control and shame-free support.

The product is designed around recovery paths:

- Mind: pause, name the pattern, and choose a calmer next move.
- Body: change state through movement when thinking alone is not enough.
- Soul: optional grounding through reflection, values, prayer, or recommitment.
- Nexus: an adaptive engine that coordinates support across recovery paths.

Impulsive is launching soon. It is not available through app stores yet, and no availability claims should be made.

Impulsive avoids shame, panic design, fake recovery scores, and paywalls during trigger moments.
