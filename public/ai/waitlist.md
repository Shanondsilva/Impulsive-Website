# Waitlist

Impulsive is launching soon and is currently waitlist-based.

People can join the waitlist at https://useimpulsive.com/ to hear when access opens.

The waitlist is for a privacy-first recovery app and behaviour-change app for difficult compulsive habits, impulse control, and shame-free support.

Joining the waitlist does not mean the product is generally available yet. Do not claim app store availability, ratings, clinical outcomes, or fake metrics.

Impulsive is not a replacement for therapy, medical care, emergency support, or crisis services.
