Getting started

The basics of setting Impulsive up.

What does Impulsive do?

Impulsive helps adults create a short pause before a selected habit takes over. When you reach for an app you have chosen to protect, Impulsive steps in with a short task or game so the next action can be more intentional.

How do I finish setting up protection?

Protection needs two things to be active:

  • Protected apps: choose which apps Impulsive watches for.
  • Usage Access: a system permission that lets Impulsive notice when a protected app opens.

If setup shows as incomplete, it almost always means Usage Access has not been granted yet. Open Settings, Protection & Focus, Usage Access and enable Impulsive in the list.

Which permissions does Impulsive ask for, and why?
  • Usage Access: to detect when a protected app comes to the foreground.
  • Notifications: to show protection status and reminders.
  • Display over other apps: so the pause screen can appear over the app you opened.
  • Battery optimization (unrestricted): so the system does not shut protection off in the background.
  • Microphone: only if you record a Future-Self message. Audio stays on your device.

Protection

How blocking and the protection monitor work.

How does Impulsive block a protected app?

While protection is on, Impulsive checks which app is in the foreground. When a protected app opens during your protected time, it brings up a pause screen with a task or game instead of letting you continue straight away.

Protection stopped working after I restarted my phone.

Some phones aggressively close background apps. To keep protection reliable, set Impulsive to Unrestricted under Settings, Protection & Focus, Battery optimization, and on certain brands (Xiaomi, Oppo, Samsung, OnePlus and similar) also allow it to auto-start in the system settings.

What is the protection monitor?

It is the part of Impulsive that runs quietly to check protected apps during your protected time. You can start or pause it from Settings, Protection & Focus.

What is uninstall protection?

An optional extra confirmation step before Impulsive can be removed, designed to add a little friction during difficult moments. It is entirely opt-in and you can turn it off at any time.

Pause windows

How timed release windows work.

What is a pause window?

Rather than blocking forever, Impulsive uses short, scheduled windows where protection eases. Completing tasks and games can adjust your next window. Each window lasts a fixed length of time.

Can I change my daily urge count?

Your daily habit-moment count is set during onboarding and is intentionally fixed afterwards, because the pause schedule is built around it. Other onboarding answers (triggers, timing, weekly target) can be reviewed in Settings, Habit setup.

Games & tasks

Pivot actions, points, and levels.

What are control points?

You earn control points by completing pivot tasks and games and by returning to plan during difficult moments. Walking away earns the most. Your totals and best sessions appear on the Score screen.

How do levels work?

Each level has a points target. As you complete tasks you fill the bar toward the next level, and your illustration grows as you progress.

What is a Future-Self message?

A short note or voice recording from you, to you, for a difficult moment. Recordings are stored privately on your device and are never uploaded.

Privacy & data

Where your information lives and how to manage it.

Where is my data stored?

Everything, including notes, sessions, and settings, stays in Impulsive's private storage on your device only. Nothing is uploaded, and other apps cannot read it.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. From Settings, Privacy & account you can export your data as a file to share wherever you like, or delete all of it from your device. Deletion asks for confirmation first and cannot be undone.

What does "Hide sensitive notifications" do?

When on, Impulsive still notifies you, but hides the details on your lock screen so a passer-by cannot read them.

Account & billing

Sign-in and Impulsive Plus.

How do I sign in?

You can continue as a guest, or sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook. Signing in lets you keep your progress safe.

What is included in Impulsive Plus?

Plus unlocks stronger tools, including additional paths, Temperature Focus, premium pivot games, deeper weekly insights, and website protection (DNS-based filtering, stronger anti-bypass tools, and future cloud protection).

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings, Impulsive Plus, Restore purchases. This becomes available once billing is connected to your account.

Troubleshooting

Common fixes.

The pause screen does not appear when I open a protected app.

Check three things: Usage Access is enabled, "Display over other apps" is allowed, and battery optimization is set to Unrestricted for Impulsive. All three are in Settings, Protection & Focus or your phone's system settings.

I am not getting notifications.

Make sure notifications are allowed for Impulsive in Settings, Privacy & account, Notifications, and that Do Not Disturb is not suppressing them.
