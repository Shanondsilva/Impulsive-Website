Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Impulsive handles information when you use the Impulsive Android application or visit useimpulsive.com.

Impulsive is operated by IMPULSIVE LTD, company number 17324160. References to "Impulsive", "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to IMPULSIVE LTD.

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool designed to help adults create a pause between a difficult habit moment and their next action. The app is designed to keep sensitive behavioural and recovery information on your device by default. Some features you choose to use, including optional cloud recovery, account services, subscriptions, crash diagnostics, and Website Protection, involve limited processing or transfer of information off the device as described below.

Information you provide when creating or using an account

Impulsive supports account access through Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, account information may include:

  • Your email address.
  • Your display name, where provided by your sign-in provider.
  • A Firebase user identifier (UID).
  • Your authentication provider, such as Google, Facebook, email, or guest access.
  • Your email verification and authentication status.

If you use Google or Facebook sign-in, the relevant provider processes your sign-in request according to its own privacy practices. Impulsive does not receive or store your Google or Facebook password.

If you create an email and password account, authentication credentials are handled through Firebase Authentication. We do not store your password in the Impulsive app database.

Behavioural and recovery information

The app may allow you to create or generate information such as recovery sessions, urge or habit-moment records, intensity ratings, reflections, journal notes, checklist items, scores, progress information, focus sessions, game activity, preferences, and related app state.

This information is stored locally on your device by default using local app storage, including an encrypted local database where applicable. Impulsive does not routinely synchronise your journal entries, recovery history, behavioural records, or personal reflections to Cloud Firestore.

If you deliberately enable the optional cloud-recovery feature, selected recovery information is copied into an encrypted recovery backup and sent off the device as described in the next section. This is separate from the app's ordinary local storage.

Backup, restore, export, and optional cloud recovery

Impulsive provides manual backup, restore, and export functionality. A manual Impulsive backup is encrypted using a password you choose. You choose where the exported file is saved and are responsible for keeping both the file and its password secure.

Selected app settings and restore information may also be eligible for Android's device backup mechanisms if backup is enabled for your device and Google account. Impulsive's Android backup rules are configured so that cloud backup is used only where the Android platform provides the required client-side encryption capability. Android device-to-device transfer is handled by the Android platform.

Cloud recovery is an optional feature for eligible signed-in, non-guest accounts. It does not turn on merely because you use Impulsive. If you enable it, you choose a separate recovery password. The app creates the recovery copy on the device and encrypts it before upload. The recovery password is used on the device for the recovery encryption process and is not included in the uploaded recovery file.

The encrypted cloud-recovery copy may include journal notes, checklist items, recovery sessions, user-added blocked domains, and certain completed onboarding information needed to restore supported app state. It does not represent a general server-side copy of everything stored by the app.

Where your Impulsive account is linked to Google, the encrypted recovery copy is stored in the Impulsive app-data area of your Google Drive account. For another eligible signed-in, non-guest account, the encrypted recovery copy may instead be stored in Firebase Storage under that account's Firebase UID. These routes are used to provide the recovery feature you choose to enable.

Turning cloud recovery off stops future scheduled cloud-recovery uploads but does not, by itself, remove an encrypted recovery copy that was already stored. Account and backup deletion are described below.

Protection features, installed apps, and Usage Access

If you choose to enable app protection features, Impulsive may use Android Usage Access to determine which app is currently in the foreground so that it can identify attempts to open apps you have chosen to protect.

The app may also read information about launchable applications installed on your device so that you can select which apps you want to protect. Selected protected-app identifiers and related protection settings are processed and stored locally on your device.

Impulsive does not use Usage Access to create advertising profiles and does not sell information about your app usage. Foreground-app information used for the protection feature is not uploaded to our servers as behavioural tracking data.

Website Protection and local VPN

If you enable Website Protection, Impulsive uses Android's VPN framework to operate an on-device DNS filtering service. The VPN is used to inspect DNS requests so that selected or predefined domains can be blocked and certain protection rules can be applied.

Impulsive does not operate a remote VPN server and does not route your general internet traffic through an Impulsive server. DNS requests that are not blocked may be forwarded over encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS connections to external family-safety DNS resolvers, including Cloudflare and AdGuard, for normal name resolution and applicable family-safety responses.

Those external DNS providers process the DNS requests needed to answer them according to their own services and privacy practices. Impulsive does not receive or keep a server-side copy of your general web-browsing history or DNS-query history through Website Protection.

Subscription and purchase information

Impulsive Plus subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment method and transaction. Impulsive does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

To verify and maintain your Plus entitlement, the app and our backend may process your Firebase UID, subscription product, entitlement status, subscription expiry information, and Google Play purchase-token information used for verification. The backend may retain a protected representation of purchase-token information and entitlement state so that a subscription can be verified and associated with the correct Impulsive account.

Security and app-integrity information

We use security services including Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity to help confirm that requests to protected backend functions come from authentic instances of the Impulsive app and to help prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access.

Crash and diagnostic information

Impulsive uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, application errors, non-fatal exceptions, and stability problems.

Diagnostic information processed by Crashlytics may include crash stack traces, error information, app and device information, operating-system information, and installation identifiers.

We do not use Crashlytics to intentionally upload your journal entries, personal reflections, recovery history, or habit-moment records.

Website and waitlist information

If you join an Impulsive waitlist or submit a form on useimpulsive.com, we may collect the email address you provide and limited technical information needed to operate the website securely and prevent spam or abuse.

Website infrastructure may be provided through Cloudflare services. Transactional or product-related emails may be delivered using an email service provider such as Brevo.

How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and manage your account.
  • Provide features you choose to use, including optional encrypted cloud recovery.
  • Verify and maintain subscription entitlements.
  • Protect the app and backend from fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical failures.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.
  • Meet applicable legal, security, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. Impulsive does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use your behavioural or recovery information for targeted advertising.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase for authentication, backend functions, App Check, Crashlytics, and Firebase Storage used for optional encrypted cloud recovery on eligible accounts.
  • Google Play for distribution, billing, subscriptions, and Play Integrity.
  • Google for Google sign-in and, where you enable it, storage of an encrypted cloud-recovery copy in the Impulsive app-data area of your Google Drive account.
  • Meta/Facebook if you choose Facebook sign-in.
  • Cloudflare and AdGuard for encrypted DNS resolution used by Website Protection.
  • Cloudflare for website infrastructure.
  • Brevo or another email provider for requested product communications.

How long we keep information

Information stored only on your device remains there until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or remove it through an available app feature, subject to any separate Android backup you have enabled.

Account and subscription information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, verify entitlements, prevent fraud, and meet legal or accounting obligations.

An encrypted cloud-recovery copy may remain in the applicable cloud-storage location until it is replaced by a later backup or removed through the relevant deletion process. Turning cloud recovery off stops future scheduled uploads but does not by itself delete an existing cloud-recovery file.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Impulsive account from within the app. You can also request account deletion without having the app installed by visiting useimpulsive.com/delete-account.

Account deletion removes the Impulsive authentication account and associated server-side records that we control, including applicable account and subscription records. For Firebase Storage cloud recovery, the deletion process removes the encrypted recovery object associated with the Firebase account.

Where an encrypted cloud-recovery copy is held in the Impulsive app-data area of your Google Drive account, the in-app account-deletion flow is designed to request the Google authorisation needed to remove that recovery copy before the Impulsive account is deleted. If you request deletion only through the website, we may not have the Google authorisation needed to access an app-data file held in your Google account. In that situation, you may need to remove Impulsive app data through the controls provided by your Google account.

Local app data can be removed through the app's data-deletion features or by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Manual backup files that you exported are under your control and must be deleted by you from the location where you saved them. Separate Android platform backups may also need to be removed through your Google or device backup settings.

We may retain limited information only where retention is necessary for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reason. Where a legal retention requirement applies, retained information is not kept for unrelated product use.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact hello@useimpulsive.com.

If UK data-protection law applies to you and you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal information, you may also make a complaint to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect the information we process, including local encrypted storage where applicable, encryption of optional cloud-recovery payloads before upload, secure authentication, encrypted network connections, app-integrity checks, and server-side purchase verification.

Children

Impulsive is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Health and crisis support

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool. It is not a medical device, therapy service, diagnosis tool, crisis-support service, or clinically validated treatment. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent addiction, compulsions, mental health conditions, or any medical condition.

If you feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else, or you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services or appropriate local crisis support immediately.
