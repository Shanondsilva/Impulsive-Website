Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Who we are

Impulsive is operated by IMPULSIVE LTD, company number 17324160. References to “Impulsive”, “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy refer to IMPULSIVE LTD. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@useimpulsive.com.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Impulsive Android application, use features connected to an Impulsive account, or visit useimpulsive.com.

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool for adults. Sensitive behavioural and recovery information is designed to remain on your device by default. Some features that you choose to use require limited off-device processing. Those features and data flows are described below.

Account information

Impulsive supports account access through Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, we may process:

  • Your email address.
  • Your display name where supplied by the sign-in provider.
  • A Firebase user identifier (UID).
  • Your authentication provider and account-verification state.

If you use Google or Facebook sign-in, that provider processes the sign-in request under its own privacy terms. Impulsive does not receive or store your Google or Facebook password. If you use email and password authentication, Firebase Authentication handles the authentication credential. Your password is not stored in the Impulsive application database.

Behavioural and recovery information

Depending on the features you use, Impulsive may allow you to create or generate information such as Moment Plans, difficult-moment or recovery-session records, intervention choices, lifecycle states, intensity ratings, feedback, later observations, journal notes, checklist items, progress information, focus sessions, game activity, protection settings and related application state.

This information is stored locally on your device by default using application storage, including encrypted local database storage where applicable. Impulsive does not routinely synchronise your readable journal entries, behavioural history or private reflections to a general cloud database.

Information that may be particularly sensitive

A user may choose to record information that is highly personal. Depending on what a person writes or records, this may reveal or permit inferences about matters such as health, sex life, sexual orientation, religious or philosophical beliefs. UK data protection law treats some of these categories as special category personal data.

Impulsive does not use behavioural, recovery or journal content for targeted advertising. Optional features that can move an encrypted recovery copy off the device are user-controlled and are described below. Where UK data protection law requires an additional condition for processing special category data, IMPULSIVE LTD will only use the information where an applicable condition and appropriate safeguards are in place.

Manual backup, export and optional cloud recovery

Impulsive can provide manual backup, restore and export functions. A manual Impulsive backup is encrypted using a password you choose. You choose where the exported file is saved and are responsible for keeping the file and password secure.

Selected application settings and restore information may also be eligible for Android platform backup mechanisms where those mechanisms are enabled on your device. Android device-to-device transfer and platform backup are operated by the Android platform and your relevant Google or device account settings.

Cloud recovery is optional. It does not turn on simply because you use Impulsive. For eligible signed-in, non-guest accounts, you can choose to enable it and create a separate recovery password. The application creates the recovery copy on the device and encrypts it before upload. The recovery password is not included in the uploaded recovery file.

An encrypted cloud-recovery copy may include supported journal notes, checklist items, recovery-session information, user-added blocked domains and selected application state needed for restoration. It is not a general readable server-side copy of everything in the application.

Where your Impulsive account is linked to Google, the encrypted recovery copy may be stored in the Impulsive app-data area of your Google Drive account. For another eligible signed-in account, the encrypted recovery copy may instead be stored in Firebase Storage under the relevant Firebase UID. These routes are used only to provide the recovery feature you choose to enable.

Turning cloud recovery off stops future scheduled recovery uploads, but does not by itself remove a recovery copy that has already been stored. Deletion is explained below.

App protection and Usage Access

If you enable app protection, Impulsive may use Android Usage Access to identify which application is in the foreground so that it can recognise attempts to open applications you have chosen to protect. The application may also read information about launchable applications installed on your device so that you can select protected applications.

Protected-app identifiers and related protection settings are processed and stored locally. Impulsive does not use foreground-app information to create advertising profiles and does not upload it to our servers as behavioural tracking data.

Website Protection and local VPN

If you enable Website Protection, Impulsive can use Android's VPN framework to operate an on-device DNS filtering service. The local VPN is used to inspect DNS requests so selected or predefined domains can be blocked and relevant protection rules applied.

Impulsive does not operate a remote VPN server and does not route your general internet traffic through an Impulsive server. DNS requests that are not blocked may be forwarded over encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS connections to external DNS resolvers, including Cloudflare and AdGuard, for name resolution and applicable family-safety responses. Those providers process the requests required to provide their DNS service under their own privacy practices.

Impulsive does not receive or retain a server-side copy of your general browsing history or DNS-query history through Website Protection.

Subscriptions and purchases

Impulsive Plus subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing where offered. Google processes the payment method and transaction. Impulsive does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

To verify and maintain an entitlement, the application and our backend may process your Firebase UID, subscription product, entitlement status, expiry information and Google Play purchase-token information needed for verification. The backend may retain a protected representation of purchase-token information and entitlement state so that a purchase can be associated with the correct Impulsive account.

Optional rewarded advertising in builds where it is enabled

Some Impulsive builds may include an optional rewarded-ad route connected to Safe Browse or another clearly identified feature. Where a released build enables this route, Impulsive may use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to load the advertisement and, where required, collect or respect advertising-consent choices.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, Google settings and the advertising configuration, Google's advertising technology may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, consent signals, diagnostic information and ad interaction information. Google acts under its own terms and privacy practices for the services it provides.

Impulsive does not provide your journal entries, Moment Plans, recovery history, difficult-moment records, protected-app selections or browsing history to Google for the purpose of selecting targeted advertisements. A rewarded advertisement is optional and is not the basis on which Impulsive interprets whether a behavioural intervention worked.

If the released application does not enable the rewarded-ad route, this advertising processing does not occur merely because it is described in this policy.

Security and app-integrity information

Impulsive uses security services including Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity where configured to help protect backend functions, verify authentic application requests and reduce fraud, abuse and unauthorised access. These services may process technical, device or integrity signals required to provide those security functions.

Crash and diagnostic information

Impulsive uses Firebase Crashlytics where enabled to identify crashes, application errors and stability problems. Diagnostic information may include stack traces, error information, application and device information, operating-system information and installation identifiers.

We do not intentionally place readable journal entries, private reflections, Moment Plan content or recovery-history content into Crashlytics reports.

Website and historical waitlist information

If you previously joined the Impulsive waitlist, contacted us or submitted a form on useimpulsive.com, we may process the email address or information you provided and limited technical information needed to operate the website securely and prevent spam or abuse. Website infrastructure may use Cloudflare. Transactional or requested product communications may use an email provider such as Brevo.

Historical waitlist information is separate from application behavioural data. A waitlist signup is not treated as a customer, download, active user or purchase.

Why we use personal information

Depending on the feature and circumstances, we use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure and manage an account.
  • Provide application features you request, including optional encrypted cloud recovery.
  • Verify and maintain subscription entitlements.
  • Protect the application and backend from fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical failures.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests.
  • Operate the website and requested communications.
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, security and regulatory obligations.
  • Where an advertising-enabled build is used, provide an optional rewarded-ad function and manage consent where required.

Lawful bases under UK data protection law

Where UK data protection law applies, the lawful basis depends on the specific purpose. We may rely on performance of a contract where processing is necessary to provide a service or feature you request, legitimate interests for proportionate security, fraud-prevention, service protection and technical reliability, legal obligation where the law requires us to retain or disclose information, and consent where the relevant processing requires a user choice or consent under applicable law.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the purpose, necessity and effect on the person's rights and freedoms. Where information is special category data, an Article 6 lawful basis alone is not sufficient and an applicable additional condition and safeguards are required.

Service providers and recipients

Depending on the features you use, service providers can include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, backend functions, App Check, Crashlytics and Firebase Storage used for optional encrypted cloud recovery on eligible accounts.
  • Google Play for application distribution, billing, subscriptions, reviews and Play Integrity.
  • Google Drive where you enable an eligible encrypted cloud-recovery route using your Google account.
  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP only in builds and circumstances where the optional rewarded-ad route is enabled.
  • Meta/Facebook if you choose Facebook sign-in.
  • Cloudflare and AdGuard for relevant encrypted DNS resolution used by Website Protection.
  • Cloudflare for website infrastructure and security.
  • Brevo or another email provider for requested or transactional product communications.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell your behavioural or recovery history, and we do not provide that content to advertisers for targeted advertising.

International processing

Some service providers operate internationally. Information processed by those providers may therefore be handled outside the United Kingdom. Where IMPULSIVE LTD is responsible for an international transfer under UK data protection law, we use an available lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards as required for the transfer concerned.

How long we keep information

Information stored only on your device remains there until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application or remove it using an available feature, subject to any separate Android backup you have enabled.

Account, subscription, security and support information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant service, entitlement, fraud-prevention, support, legal or accounting purpose.

An encrypted cloud-recovery copy may remain in its cloud-storage location until it is replaced or removed through the relevant deletion process. Turning cloud recovery off stops future scheduled uploads but does not by itself delete an existing cloud copy.

Historical waitlist information is retained only for the purpose for which it was collected and can be removed on request unless a limited retention obligation applies.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Impulsive account from within the application. You can also request account deletion without the application installed by visiting useimpulsive.com/delete-account.

Account deletion removes the Impulsive authentication account and associated server-side records that we control, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain for a limited legal, security, fraud-prevention or accounting reason. Where Firebase Storage holds an encrypted recovery object associated with the Firebase account, the deletion process is designed to remove that object.

Where an encrypted recovery copy is held in the Impulsive app-data area of your Google Drive account, the in-app deletion flow is designed to request the Google authorisation needed to remove that copy before the Impulsive account is deleted. A website-only deletion request may not give us permission to access app data held within your Google account. In that case you may need to remove Impulsive app data using the controls in your Google account.

Local application data can be removed through available data-deletion features, by clearing app storage or by uninstalling the application. Manual backup files you exported remain under your control and must be deleted by you from the location where you saved them. Separate Android platform backups may also need to be managed through your Google or device backup settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal of consent does not make processing that occurred before withdrawal unlawful.

To make a privacy request, contact hello@useimpulsive.com.

If UK data protection law applies and you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal information, you can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect the information we process. Depending on the feature, these include encrypted local storage, encryption of optional recovery payloads before upload, secure authentication, encrypted network connections, access controls, app-integrity checks and server-side purchase verification. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Impulsive is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Health and crisis support

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool. It is not a medical device, therapy service, diagnosis tool, crisis-support service or clinically validated treatment. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent addiction, compulsions, mental-health conditions or any medical condition.

If you are in immediate danger or believe that you may harm yourself or someone else, contact the appropriate emergency or crisis service where you are located.
