Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026

About this notice

Impulsive is an early-stage, pre-launch behaviour-change support tool. The product is currently in waitlist phase and is not yet generally available. This page explains how we handle personal information collected through useimpulsive.com during this pre-launch period.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you submit. We may also process technical information that is needed to operate the site securely and prevent spam, including a one-way hashed form of your IP address, your browser user agent, the referring page, and the page you submitted from.

We do not collect any behavioural recovery data, urge ratings, or reflections through this website. Those features belong to the Impulsive app, which is not yet released.

How we use your information

Your email is used to send you early access, testing, and launch updates about Impulsive. Technical information is used to keep the site working, prevent abuse of the waitlist form, and understand the source of signups in aggregate.

We do not sell personal data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Where your information is stored

Waitlist signups may be stored in Cloudflare D1 (or equivalent backend storage) and processed through Cloudflare Workers. Transactional confirmation email may be sent through an email provider such as Brevo, only for the purpose of confirming your signup and sending product updates.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information until launch, plus a reasonable period to send launch communications. If you ask us to remove your email earlier, we will do so.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to remove your email from the waitlist or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. To do this, email hello@useimpulsive.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, or object to the processing of your data.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited information collected through the site. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have a security concern, please contact hello@useimpulsive.com.

Children

Impulsive is not directed at children. Please do not submit a waitlist signup on behalf of anyone under 16.

Important note about health and crisis support

Impulsive is a behaviour-change support tool. It is not a medical device, therapy, diagnosis, clinical treatment, or a crisis service, and it does not provide medical advice. If you are in crisis or need urgent help, please contact local emergency services, your GP, a qualified therapist, or a crisis service such as the Samaritans on 116 123.

Changes to this notice

We may update this page as the product develops. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.

Contact

Questions about privacy or removal requests can be sent to hello@useimpulsive.com.
